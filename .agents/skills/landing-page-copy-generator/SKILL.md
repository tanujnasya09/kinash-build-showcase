# Landing Page Copy Generator

Source: WesleySmits/agent-skills & marketing-skills.com

## When to use this skill

- Writing landing page copy and value propositions
- Crafting high-converting hero section content (eyebrow, headline, subheadline, CTAs)
- Optimizing Call-To-Action (CTA) buttons with action + outcome verbs
- Structuring conversion-focused sections (Problem, Solution, Proof, Offer, Guarantee)
- Reviewing and upgrading website marketing copy without changing UI design

## High-Converting Copy Frameworks

### 1. The Hero Section Formula (Above the Fold)
1. **Eyebrow / Badge**: Immediate category anchor + credibility (e.g. `PRIME FOOTHILL ESTATES • 100% CLEAR TITLE REGISTRY`).
2. **Primary Headline (H1)**: Outcome or identity-driven statement (not generic clichés like "Building Excellence").
   * *Formula A*: [Desirable Outcome] Without [Common Fear/Pain].
   * *Formula B*: The [Category] Built for [Specific Aspirational Identity].
   * *Formula C*: Architectural Distinction Meets Generational Capital Growth.
3. **Subheadline (Lede)**: 1-2 punchy sentences answering:
   * What is it?
   * Who is it for?
   * What tangible transformation or safety does it provide?
4. **Primary CTA**: Value/Benefit action (Avoid passive words like "Submit", "Read More", "About Us").
   * *Examples*: "View Available Portfolios", "Request Private Dossier", "Schedule Advisory Session".
5. **Secondary CTA**: Low-friction discovery action.
   * *Examples*: "Explore Interactive Price Map", "Calculate Investment Yield", "Review Verification Audits".
6. **Social Proof / Risk Reversal Micro-copy**:
   * E.g. "ISO 9001:2015 Accredited • Direct Registry Transfer • No Middleman Markups".

### 2. High-Converting CTA Button Rules
* **First-Person or Direct-Action Intent**: Use verbs that imply receiving something desirable.
* **Never use dead-end labels**: Replace "About Us" with "Why Leading Investors Trust Us" or "Our Track Record".
* **Specific Friction Reducers**: Include micro-labels below or inside CTAs (e.g., "Direct Partner Line", "Zero Obligation").

### 3. Section Messaging Architecture
* **Stats Section**: Frame raw numbers around client outcomes (Capital Deployed, Clear Title Deeds Transfer, Zero Structural Disputes).
* **Bento / Features**: Transform features into unfair advantages.
* **Closing Conversion Section**: Create a decisive, elegant invitation with a clear next step.
